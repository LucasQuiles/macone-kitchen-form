## 1. SCOPE OF WORK
Contractor will furnish the labor, supervision, tools, and equipment to perform the following Work at the Property ("Work"):

**(a) Structural** — Remove two (2) existing load-bearing interior kitchen walls to create an open floor plan; provide temporary shoring during removal; install engineered support beam(s) and posts/bearing per a New York State licensed professional engineer's stamped design.

**(b) Permits & Engineering** — Coordinate and obtain the required building permit and the licensed structural engineer's stamped beam design (Owner-paid pass-through costs — see §4).

**(c) Electrical** — Relocate and re-wire to code any outlets, switches, and wiring located within the removed walls.

**(d) Drywall & Patch** — Close, tape, mud, and finish the ceiling and adjacent wall surfaces to a paint-ready condition where the walls were removed.

**(e) Flooring** — Patch and infill the flooring in the footprint of the removed walls.

**(f) Cabinetry** — Remove and haul away all existing kitchen cabinets; furnish installation labor for the new Owner-purchased Home Depot cabinets (approximately ______ linear feet).

**Exclusions.** Any work not expressly listed above is excluded, including but not limited to: countertops, backsplash, appliances, plumbing relocation, final painting/priming, and any asbestos, lead, or mold abatement — unless added by a signed written Change Order.
