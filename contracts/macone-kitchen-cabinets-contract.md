## 1. SCOPE OF WORK
Contractor will furnish the labor, supervision, tools, and equipment to perform the following Work at the Property ("Work"):

**(a) Cabinet Removal** — Remove and haul away the existing kitchen cabinets on the two affected walls (approximately **26 linear feet** total: one wall of approx. 12 ft and one wall of approx. 14 ft).

**(b) Cabinet Installation** — Furnish installation labor for the new Owner-purchased Home Depot cabinets (uppers and lowers) on those two walls — set level, plumb, secured to studs, and doors/drawers adjusted.

**Exclusions.** Any work not expressly listed above is excluded, including but not limited to: wall removal or structural work, countertops, backsplash, appliances, sink/plumbing, electrical, flooring, drywall, painting, and any asbestos, lead, or mold abatement — unless added by a signed written Change Order.

## 2. MATERIALS — OWNER-SUPPLIED
**(a)** Owner shall purchase and pay for **ALL materials directly from The Home Depot**, in full and in advance of the Work. This includes the cabinets, filler/trim pieces, fasteners, and any hardware.

**(b)** Contractor is **not responsible** for the cost, price changes, availability, delivery timing, defects, or warranty of Owner-supplied materials. Work delayed by late, insufficient, back-ordered, or defective Owner-supplied materials extends the completion date day-for-day and may be billed as a Change Order for any return trips or remobilization.

**(c)** Manufacturer warranties on all materials run to Owner. Contractor warrants only its installation workmanship (see §8).

**(d)** Owner shall ensure the required cabinets and materials are delivered and on site before Work begins.

## 8. LIMITED WARRANTY
Contractor warrants its **installation workmanship** against defects for **one (1) year** from Substantial Completion. Contractor's sole obligation is to correct defective workmanship at no labor charge; Owner supplies any replacement materials (materials being Owner-purchased). This warranty **excludes** Owner-supplied material defects (covered by manufacturer), normal wear, settlement/movement, alteration or misuse by Owner or others, lack of maintenance, and damage from conditions outside Contractor's Work. This is the only warranty and, to the extent allowed by law, replaces all implied warranties.
